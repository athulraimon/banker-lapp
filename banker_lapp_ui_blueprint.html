🎨 Design Foundations & Token Guide

The Aesthetic

High-contrast Formula 1 trackside branding on a premium carbon dark-mode surface, engineered for mobile (Expo / React Native, with a phone-width web build).

Color Tokens

Base Carbon #0b0c10 · Accent Red #e10600 · Teal #00f5d4 · Gold #ffb703

Typography

Headings: Space Grotesk 700 (geometric, performance-tuned).
Body / UI: Outfit 400. Fonts load with a 3s timeout, falling back to system fonts so a slow load never blocks the app.

Motion & Loading NEW

Every data screen opens on a shaped skeleton, resolves with a staggered fade-in-up cascade, and gives spring scale feedback on press. Full-screen waits use a branded animated loader. Built on RN's Animated API — no business logic touched.

Status legend
Predictions Open (before FP1) Locked (FP1 → race) Provisional (results settling) Finished

Authentication

Google-only sign-in. The session is restored on launch before any routing decision, so a returning user is never bounced to login.

How Banker Lapp works

Predict the front of the grid. Beat your mates over a season.

1
Pick four drivers
Open the next Grand Prix and choose who takes pole, plus your top three finishers.
2
Lock in before Practice 1
Change your picks as often as you like until FP1 starts. After that the race is locked.
3
Collect points
Once the results are in, exact matches score. Points add up across the season.
Points
PoleWho takes pole position5
P1Race winner15
P2Second place10
P3Third place8

Only exact matches score. Your pole pick can also be your P1, but P1, P2 and P3 must be three different drivers.

Timing
OpensAs soon as the previous race finishes
LocksThe moment Practice 1 starts

Schedule and driver data from the Jolpica F1 API. Circuit layouts from the f1-circuits dataset (MIT). Unofficial and not associated with Formula 1.
